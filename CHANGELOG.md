# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [1.3.0] - 2026-10-22

- **Lists instead of one per line:** a threat's grim portents and stakes, a place's features and a session's potential scenes are now lists built a row at a time, like truths: a box for each, buttons to move or remove it, and Add.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-21

- **Encounters:** a quest's plan is now its encounters, in order, instead of one list of monsters. Each is combat, social, exploration, puzzle, trap or other, with what happens, its monsters (with how many), the NPCs in it, its own maps and its own reward (items with how many, coins and XP). Add, move and remove them on the quest's plan; each has its own page in a new Encounters journal, and the quest's page lists them. A quest's monsters from before are moved into an encounter called "Encounter" (for a Dungeon Master Guru campaign, when the website moves them and you Sync).
- **Encounter difficulty:** a combat encounter shows its monsters' XP against the party's 2024 XP budget (Low, Moderate and High), on its card, its journal page and the Dashboard.
- **Handouts:** drag journal entries or pages from the Journal sidebar or a compendium onto a quest's Handouts. The quest's page links them, and the Dashboard's **Show to players** button shows one to the players you choose.
- **Running encounters:** an active quest on the Dashboard shows each encounter's monsters to drag onto a map, its maps to view or activate, and a **Give the reward** button for its reward.
- **Random encounter tables:** drop roll tables on a place in the Places tab. **Roll** (in the planner, and on the Dashboard for an active quest's place or the next session's places) draws from one for the GMs only. The place's journal page links them.
- **Homebrew monsters on Dungeon Master Guru:** in the Connect window's Compendiums section, tick this world's NPC actors or your own Actor compendiums to pick them on the website too. Only names, CRs and IDs are sent, never stat blocks; untick one and the website forgets it.
- **Import monsters from Dungeon Master Guru:** a monster from the website with no compendium entry of the same name gets an **Import into this world** button in the planner and the Dashboard. It becomes an actor in a "Dungeon Master Guru" folder, and links to that monster point at it from then on.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-20

- **Truths as a list:** each truth of the setting now has a name and a description. Add them one at a time, move or remove them, and see them numbered in the planner, the New campaign guide and the Overview journal page. There's no limit of six any more (about six is still plenty), and truths saved before are kept, each split into a name and a description at its first sentence.

## [1.0.0]

The first release.

- **Plan in your world, no account needed:** the New campaign guide walks you through a campaign step by step (what kind of game, the pitch, the party, the world and its truths, your books) and makes it in this world, with a journal folder that has an entry for each section and a page for each record.
- **Campaign Planner:** plan it all in Foundry, with the same tabs as Dungeon Master Guru: Overview with Getting started, Party, Story (threats and factions), Quests, Cast, Places, Secrets with the Three Clue Rule check, and Sessions with the eight steps of prep. Add, edit and delete anything.
- **Campaign Dashboard:** run it at the table: the next session and its log, threat clocks and grim portents, secrets to reveal, and quest status. It opens when the world loads; untick **Open at startup** in its footer to stop that.
- **Quests:** each quest's maps, the NPCs involved, its monsters and its reward: items with how many, coins, and XP or a milestone. The Dashboard shows an active quest's maps, monsters and reward.
- **Give the reward:** from the Dashboard, hand a quest's reward or a session's treasure to the characters: choose who gets each item, share the coins evenly, and share the XP or give it to each. Items join a stack the character already has, and the chat lists who got what. With Item Piles set up, leave it on the scene as an item pile instead.
- **Maps, actors and treasure:** drag scenes onto places, sessions and quests, and monsters, items and roll tables in from your compendiums. Drop an actor on the Cast tab to add an NPC (a hostile token starts as an Enemy, a friendly one as an Ally). The Dashboard shows a map to you or activates it for the players, and items drag onto a character's sheet.
- **Switch off what you don't use:** in Module Settings, turn off the six truths, threat clocks, grim portents, secrets and clues, the Three Clue Rule check or the Getting started checklist. The planner, Dashboard, New campaign guide and journal pages stop showing it; nothing is deleted, and it all comes back when you switch it on again.
- **Credits:** small **Learn more** links next to each technique point to where it comes from (Sly Flourish, The Alexandrian, Dungeon World, Blades in the Dark and Johnn Four), and the How it works window and README list them all.
- **Export as an Adventure:** pack a campaign's journals, maps and actors into a Foundry Adventure in a world compendium (**Export as Adventure** in the Campaign Planner). Imported into a world, it opens as a campaign of its own, no account needed. Exporting again replaces the earlier export.
- **Easy to find:** Plan, Run, New, Switch and help buttons at the top of the Journal sidebar; Switch is also at the top of the Campaign Planner and Dashboard, and a How it works window.
- **Dungeon Master Guru (optional):** connect with a code, with no API token to copy, to plan from any computer or phone. Make a campaign there from the New campaign guide, or send this world's campaign with **Send to Dungeon Master Guru**.
- **Two-way sync:** link a Dungeon Master Guru campaign and it comes into your journals. Change it here in the Campaign Planner and Dashboard or on the website. Changes made here are saved in the world straight away, even with no connection, and **Sync** (at the top of the Campaign Planner and Dashboard) sends them and brings back the website's; nothing syncs on its own, with a choice when the same thing changed in both. If Dungeon Master Guru can't be reached, nothing is lost: the changes wait for the next Sync.
- **Your books, party and maps on Dungeon Master Guru:** with sync, the module sends the names, CRs and IDs (never stat blocks) of your SRD 5.2 and 2024 core book compendiums, your player characters' sheets (names of spells, features and gear, not their text), and a thumbnail of each scene, so you can plan with them on the website. Monsters, items and tables picked there are compendium links in your journals, ready to drag onto a scene.
- **Welcome and what's new:** a chat message welcomes the GM on first install and lists what's new after each feature update.
- **Report a bug:** in the Dashboard's footer, beside links to Patreon and Dungeon Master Guru, and in the window menus. It opens a GitHub issue with your Foundry, system and module versions filled in.
