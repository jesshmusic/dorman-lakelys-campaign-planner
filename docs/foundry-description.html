Plan your campaign in Foundry and run it at the table. The prep follows Sly Flourish's Return of the Lazy Dungeon Master: a short brief, threats with clocks, secrets and clues, and eight quick steps for each session.

  • New campaign guide. Pick a story arc, a sandbox or a one-shot and answer a few questions. It sets up the sessions, acts and main threat for you.
  • Campaign Planner. The party, threats, quests, cast, places, secrets and sessions in one window. It's all saved as journal pages, so you can share them with your players.
  • Campaign Dashboard. The next session and its log, threat clocks, secrets to reveal, quest status, and a button to hand out rewards.
  • Uses your books. Drag monsters, items and roll tables in from your compendiums, and maps from the Scenes directory.
  • No account needed. If you want to plan from your phone or another computer, connect to Dungeon Master Guru (free) and click Sync when you're ready.

For D&D 5e. Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. If you'd like to support it, I'm on Patreon.
